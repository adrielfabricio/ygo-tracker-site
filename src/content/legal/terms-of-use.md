## What the app does

YGO Tracker helps you track a physical Yu-Gi-Oh! card collection: decks with needed and owned
quantities, set collections, a wishlist, and estimated costs. It also offers a profile you can make public, with the decks and collections you choose to share, and a Life Point Counter that can record duels between two players. It is a personal tracking tool. It
does not sell cards, broker trades, or guarantee the accuracy of any price or card data.

## Life Point Counter and paired duels

The Life Point Counter works on one phone and records nothing. Two players at the same table can pair their phones to record a duel: the player who keeps the Life Points sends the result, and it counts on either player's history and scoreboard only after the other player confirms it on their phone. The other player can dispute it instead, and a disputed result does not count. A confirmed result is final.

The scoreboard is a record between players who agreed on each result. There is no ranking and no prize, and we do not guarantee that any scoreboard, history or badge is accurate.
