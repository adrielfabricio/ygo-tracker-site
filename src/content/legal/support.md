# Support — YGO Tracker

**Last updated**: 2026-11-23

## Get in touch

Questions, bugs, or suggestions: **adrielfabricio.dev@gmail.com**

I reply within a few business days. It helps a lot if you mention your device, the app version
(Settings → About → Version), and what you were doing when the problem showed up.

## Frequently asked

### I bought Lifetime Access and it did not unlock

Open **Settings → Premium → Restore purchases**. The purchase belongs to the store account you
bought it with, so sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

If it still does not come back, email me with the purchase date and the email on your app
account.

### I did not get the password reset email

The message comes from **no-reply@ygotracker.app** and usually arrives within a minute. Check
your spam and promotions folders, and make sure you typed the email you signed up with. If it
still does not show up, write to the address above.

### I got a new phone. Do I lose my decks?

No. Decks, collections, and wishlist live on your account, not on the device. Sign in with the
same login and everything comes back.

### Does the app work without internet?

Mostly. You can create and edit decks, mark cards, and adjust quantities offline: in line at the shop, at a tournament, on the bus. It syncs on its own once you are back online. Card names and text come from a card catalog the app keeps on your device, so your lists show them in your language offline too. The Life Point Counter works offline, and so does pairing two phones for a duel.

Some things need a connection: searching for new cards (the card itself comes from an external database), searching duelists and opening their profiles, sending, confirming or disputing a duel result, opening your duel history, changing your username, and sending a report.

### I cannot find a card in search

Search looks the name up in a list of every card in English and Portuguese that the app downloads and keeps on your device, so you can type either name, then fetches the card from the public YGOPRODeck database. That list is refreshed every week, so a card released in the last few days may not show up yet, and some cards have no Portuguese name: if the Portuguese name finds nothing, try the English one.

### Are the prices real market values?

No. The values are the ones **you** enter, so you can estimate what is left to spend. The app
does not check shop prices and does not track the market.

### How do I delete my account and my data?

In the app: **Settings → Account → Delete account**, then confirm.

Deletion is permanent and immediate, and needs a connection. It removes your decks, collections, wishlist, the account record, your public profile and username, your scoreboard and badges, the public copies of your decks and collections, and your block list. There is no undo.

A few things stay, as the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) explains: duels the other player confirmed stay in their history without your name, reports are kept for the moderator, and anything hidden by moderation stays hidden, with its username taken, until the moderator removes it.

Lifetime Access belongs to your store account, not to your app account. If you create a new
account later and use the same store account, Restore purchases brings it back.

### What happens to my public data if I make my profile private?

Making your profile private hides it at once, together with your scoreboard, your badges and every deck and collection you made public. Nothing is deleted: make the profile public again and they all come back. To take a single deck or collection off your profile, turn off "Public on my profile" on it; that deletes its public copy. Copies other duelists already made in their own accounts are theirs and stay.

Deleting the account deletes the profile and the public copies (see the question above).

### How do I report a profile, deck or collection?

Open the duelist's profile, or their public deck or collection, and choose **Report**. You can also report an opponent from a row of your duel history. Pick a reason, add details if you want, and send. The person is never told who reported them, and there is a daily limit on reports.

### How do I block or unblock someone?

Choose **Block** on their profile, on one of their public decks or collections, or on a row of your duel history. You stop seeing them in search, on their profile and on their decks and collections, and no duel between you can be recorded. They are not told.

To unblock, open **Settings → Account → Blocked users** and tap **Unblock**, or use Unblock in your duel history.

### How do I change my username?

**Settings → Account → Profile → Edit profile**. You can change your username once every 30 days, and the screen shows when the next change is allowed. If the app picked your username for you, the first change is free, and so is a change the moderator asks for. Your duelist name can change at any time.

### Why was my profile, deck or collection hidden?

Someone reported it and the moderator found that it breaks the [Terms of Use](/terms): an offensive name or description, harassment, impersonation or spam. The app shows the reason, and you also got an email. Nothing was deleted, and other duelists cannot see it while it is hidden.

If it was hidden for your username or duelist name, change it in Edit profile: the profile stays hidden until the moderator reviews the new name. To contest the decision, write to the moderation contact, **moderation@ygotracker.app** (also in Settings → About → Moderation contact), with your username and what was hidden.

### How does a paired duel work?

Both players need the app, a connection at the end of the duel and an account with a username.

1. The player who keeps the Life Points opens the Life Point Counter, then the **Duels** menu → **Paired duel**.
2. The other player opens the same menu on their phone → **Join duel**, and shows the code.
3. The first player scans it. The counter now shows both duelists; play as usual.
4. At the end, **End duel** → **Send result**.
5. The other player confirms or disputes the result on their phone, on the Join duel screen or in their duel history.

Only a confirmed result counts, on both players' history and scoreboard. A disputed result does not count, and a confirmed one cannot be changed. If one of you blocked the other, the duel cannot be recorded. Your duel history opens from your profile.
